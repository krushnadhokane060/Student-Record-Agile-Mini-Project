# Enterprise Student Record and Academic Management Platform

## Agile Mini Project

This project focuses on the design, planning, and Agile development of an Enterprise Student Record and Academic Management Platform.

### Project Units

- Unit 1 – Design Thinking and Product Planning
- Unit 2 – Agile Mini Project

### Objective

The platform is designed to manage student records and academic information within a higher education institution.

### Main Modules

- Student Management
- Course Management
- Attendance Management
- Marks and Academic Performance
- Authentication and Security
- Notifications
- Reporting

### Methodology

The project follows Design Thinking and Agile methodology.
